# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | SQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** using **SQL and Power BI** to analyze workforce data for over **22,000 employees**.

The dashboard provides insights into workforce demographics, hiring trends, departments, states, age groups, gender, race, and work arrangements.

The project demonstrates the use of data analysis, data transformation, DAX calculations, and interactive data visualization to generate meaningful HR and workforce insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze workforce demographics
- Understand employee distribution across departments
- Analyze employee distribution by state
- Identify hiring trends over time
- Analyze gender and race distribution
- Understand employee age-group distribution
- Compare remote and headquarters work arrangements
- Generate actionable workforce insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **SQL** – Data querying and analysis
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculations and KPI creation

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Workforce Distribution

- Ohio has the highest employee concentration.
- Engineering is the largest department.
- The **30–39 age group** is the largest employee age group.
- Headquarters employees significantly exceed remote employees.

### ⚧️ Gender Distribution

- Male and female representation is relatively balanced.
- The dashboard provides a clear comparison of workforce distribution by gender.

### 🌍 Race Distribution

- Workforce composition can be analyzed across different racial groups.
- The dashboard helps identify the distribution of employees across these groups.

### 📅 Hiring Trends

- Hiring activity remained relatively stable over the analyzed period.
- Hiring activity declined toward **2020**, indicating a slowdown in hiring.

### 🏢 Work Arrangement

- **75.25%** of employees work at headquarters.
- **24.75%** of employees work remotely.
- The workforce is therefore predominantly based at headquarters.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Monitor hiring trends to identify periods of workforce growth and decline.
- Evaluate department-level workforce distribution to support resource planning.
- Focus workforce planning on high-concentration states such as Ohio.
- Continue monitoring remote and headquarters work arrangements.
- Analyze workforce demographics to support effective employee planning.
- Use hiring trends and workforce distribution to improve future recruitment strategies.
